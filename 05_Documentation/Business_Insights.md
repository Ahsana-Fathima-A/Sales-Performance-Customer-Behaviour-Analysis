# Business Insights
## Sales Performance & Customer Behaviour Analysis

This document summarizes the key analytical areas explored through the Power BI dashboard.

## 1. Overall Sales Performance

The Sales Overview page provides a consolidated view of business performance through key metrics such as:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Profit Margin
- Average Sales per Order

It also helps explore sales performance across different time periods and regions.

## 2. Product Performance

The Product Analysis page helps evaluate products and categories based on sales, profit, and quantity.

It supports the identification of:

- High-performing product categories.
- Products generating higher sales.
- Products contributing to overall profit.
- Differences in product performance.

The Product Performance drill-through page provides a more detailed view of an individual product.

## 3. Customer Behaviour

The Customer Analysis page explores customer-related metrics and purchasing patterns.

It helps analyze:

- Total number of customers.
- Total number of orders.
- Average orders per customer.
- Customer contribution to sales and profit.
- Customer purchasing trends.

The Customer Insights drill-through page provides detailed information about an individual customer.

## 4. Sales Target Performance

The Sales Target Analysis page compares actual sales against monthly sales targets.

It uses metrics such as:

- Sales Target
- Sales Variance
- Target Achievement Percentage

This analysis helps evaluate target achievement and identify differences between actual sales and planned targets.

## 5. Time-Based Analysis

The dashboard includes time-based analysis to explore sales trends across months and years.

It supports:

- Monthly sales comparisons.
- Year-over-year sales comparisons.
- Previous-year sales analysis.
- Sales growth analysis.

## 6. Interactive Analysis

The dashboard includes interactive features that allow users to explore data from different perspectives.

These include:
- Page navigation.
- Slicers and filters.
- Drill-through analysis.
- Interactive charts and tables.

## 7. Conclusion

The dashboard brings together sales, product, customer, and target performance analysis in one interactive Power BI solution.

It provides a structured way to explore business data and supports data-driven understanding of sales performance.
